📸 Pro Tips for Perfect Quote Photos

What We Need To See

  • The entire canopy: Step back far enough so the very top of the tree is visible.
  • Obstacles: Include any nearby houses, fences, sheds, or powerlines in the frame.
  • The base: A clear shot of the trunk entering the ground (helps spot root rot or tight spaces).

Common Mistakes

  • Too zoomed in: A picture of just leaves or bark doesn't help us determine the size or rigging needs.
  • Pitch black: Avoid taking photos at night without adequate lighting.
  • Hidden path: Forgetting to mention or show if the tree is in a fenced backyard with no gate access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about tree removal in Huntsville and North Alabama.

Do I need a permit to cut down a tree in Huntsville or Madison?
In most residential cases on private property outside of historic zones, no permit is required. However, there are exceptions for properties in historic districts (like Twickenham or Old Town) or specific HOA regulations (like in Hampton Cove). We can help you navigate local Huntsville City or Madison County ordinances during our quoting process.
How do you handle removals around powerlines?
If a tree is touching or dangerously close to Huntsville Utilities primary service lines, we often coordinate directly with them. We use precision rigging and controlled dismantling techniques, slowly lowering sections to the ground to ensure zero impact to your roof, fences, or vital utility infrastructure.
What happens to the wood and debris after a storm takes down my tree?
Severe "Dixie Alley" storms can create a huge mess. Our full-service removal includes complete cleanup. We chip all brush and branches, haul away the heavy logs, and rake the work area clean. We also offer options to leave firewood-length logs if requested, perfect for those chilly Appalachian foothill winters.
Is stump grinding included with tree removal?
Tree removal and stump grinding are usually quoted as separate line items because they require completely different specialized machinery (especially challenging in our hard local red clay). We offer bundled discounts if you schedule both services together!
