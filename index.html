Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about professional stump grinding and property restoration.

Stump grinding utilizes specialized carbide-tipped cutter wheels to shred the stump and root flare into wood chips, typically finishing 6 to 8 inches below soil grade. Complete extraction requires heavy excavation equipment (like a backhoe) to pull out the entire root ball, disturbing a large area and costing significantly more. Grinding is the industry standard for residential properties unless you are pouring a structural foundation.
Our standard specification is a 6 to 8-inch sub-surface grind. This thoroughly eliminates the main stump core and visible root flare, providing ample depth for topsoil and turfgrass establishment. If you require a "deep grind" (12 to 18 inches) for replanting a new caliper tree or hardscaping over the exact location, please request this during your estimate.
Do not measure just the top cut. For an accurate quote, measure the diameter at the widest point where the root flare meets the soil grade. If the stump has significant surface "finger roots" extending into the turf, please note this in your submission, as these require additional lateral grinding passes.
Yes. Our fleet features compact, rubber-tracked grinders that can articulate and pass through standard 36-inch backyard gates without turf damage. If your access is unusually tight or involves steep retaining walls, let us know so we can deploy the appropriate walk-behind unit.
Absolutely. Striking a buried line is a severe hazard. We are fully compliant with Alabama 811. We handle the utility locate tickets prior to mobilization to ensure our grinding wheel won't impact underground gas, electric, or fiber optic lines running near the root zone.
Grinding produces a mixture of fluffed wood chips and soil tailings, usually 3 to 4 times the volume of the original stump. Our standard service includes backfilling the depression with this mulch, which naturally decomposes and enriches the soil profile. We also offer full chip haul-off and topsoil grading as a premium add-on service.
For the vast majority of hardwood and softwood species in North Alabama, grinding the stump and trunk flare below grade terminates the vascular system. The remaining lateral roots will naturally decompose underground. Highly aggressive species like Chinese Tallow may occasionally send up suckers, which can be easily managed with a targeted foliar herbicide application.
Yes, we specialize in surface root eradication. We can chase and grind exposed lateral roots (often from mature Maples or Sweetgums) that are damaging your mower blades or creating tripping hazards, without necessarily removing the parent tree, provided an ISA Certified Arborist determines it won't destabilize the canopy.
